Do Threads spoiler covers keep hiding content that doesn't need to be hidden?
This tool automatically reveals all spoiler text, images, and videos, so you can read every Threads post without tapping to uncover anything.
You can also highlight revealed spoiler text with a background color to easily tell what was originally hidden.
It also adds the browser's native video controls to videos on Threads, Instagram, and Facebook Reels, so you can play, pause, seek, adjust the volume, and go fullscreen right on the video.
Set a default volume and videos start at your level instead of suddenly blasting at 100%.
On Instagram and Facebook Reels, your sound choice carries over: unmute once and the next Reel plays with sound at the same volume; mute it and the next one stays muted.
On Facebook Reels, the caption moves beside the video so it never covers the controls.
Works instantly after installation. Every feature can be toggled or customized in the settings, and video controls can be turned on or off per site (Threads is on by default; turn on Instagram and Facebook in the settings).

Threads 的防劇透功能常常遮住根本不需要隱藏的內容嗎？
這款工具會自動展開所有被遮住的文字、照片與影片，讓你每一則 Threads 都能順暢閱讀，不再手動點開。
你還可以為被揭開的劇透文字加上背景色標記，輕鬆分辨哪些是原本被隱藏的內容。
此外，它也能為 Threads、Instagram 與 Facebook Reels 的影片啟用瀏覽器原生的播放控制列，直接在影片上播放、暫停、拖曳進度、調整音量與全螢幕。
你可以設定預設音量，影片會以你設定的音量播放，不會突然以 100% 音量爆音。
在 Instagram 與 Facebook Reels 上，聲音設定會延續：取消靜音後，下一支 Reel 也會以相同音量播放；設為靜音，下一支也會維持靜音。
在 Facebook Reels 上，貼文內文會移到影片旁邊，不再遮住控制列。
安裝後即可自動運作，所有功能都可以在設定中個別開關與調整，影片控制列也能依網站分別啟用（預設只開啟 Threads，Instagram 與 Facebook 可在設定中開啟）。
